📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

From career milestones to new music releases to major announcements and those little important moments, Billboard editors highlight uplifting moments in Latin music. Here’s what happened in the Latin music world this week.

Valentín Elizalde’s Story and Legacy to Be Revisited in New HBO Max Docuseries

HBO Max announced on Wednesday (Oct. 28) a three-part documentary series, Valentín: La muerte del Gallo de Oro, revisiting the life and 2006 killing of regional Mexican music and banda superstar Valentín Elizalde. Set to premiere Nov. 12, the series will draw on archival material and new voices to revisit questions surrounding the case and the enduring legacy of “El Gallo de Oro.”

The announcement has also raised questions about the family’s involvement: Elizalde’s daughter Valentina said publicly that the family had not known about the series and had not authorized it. The project offers a chance to revisit Elizalde’s place in música mexicana, while his family’s concerns remain an important part of the story.

Ozuna Announces One-Night Concert at Puerto Rico’s Coliseo

After wrapping his Una Aventura World Tour, Ozuna announced on Tuesday (Oct. 27) that he will return home for Sempiterno, a one-night concert at Puerto Rico’s Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot on Dec. 26. The show will celebrate his catalog of hits while offering fans a preview of his next musical chapter.

“I’ve been wanting to come back and perform for my people in Puerto Rico for a long time,” the singer said in a statement. “Performing at the Choliseo always means so much to me, and this is my way of thanking my fans for all the love and support they’ve shown me since day one.” Tickets are now on sale through Ticketera.

Becky G, Draco Rosa and Jaime Gilinski to Be Honored at Maestro Cares Gala

Becky G, Draco Rosa and Colombian business leader Jaime Gilinski will be honored at the 13th annual Maestro Cares Foundation “Changing Lives, Building Dreams” Gala on Nov. 4 at Cipriani Wall Street in New York. The foundation, co-founded by Marc Anthony and music executive Henry Cárdenas, raises funds for programs supporting children and communities across Latin America and the U.S.

Becky G will receive the Voice of Change Award for her advocacy and work supporting education, immigrant families and women and Latino communities. Rosa will receive the Courage to Care Award for his efforts to support people and families facing serious health challenges, while Gilinski will be presented with the Corporate Social Responsibility Award for his business leadership and philanthropy. Marc Anthony is set to perform at the gala.

Rosalía Honored as WSJ. Magazine’s Music Innovator, Sends Support to Spain

Rosalía was named Music Innovator at the 16th annual WSJ. Magazine Innovator Awards in New York on Thursday (Oct. 29), joining honorees across fashion, tech, literature, film, sports and comedy. During her acceptance speech, she also paused to acknowledge the deadly train crash in her native Spain and offer support to those affected.

“I would like to send all my love and support to each and every person affected by it,” she said in Spanish. Sheffield Wednesday face Blackpool on Sunday, and young centre-back Ernie Weaver could start again as defensive injury issues linger.

Sheffield Wednesday come into Saturday’s clash with Blackpool looking for a first win in three League One games. Henrik Pedersen was sacked by SWFC after 2-0 losses to Notts County and Doncaster Rovers, and Matt Hamshaw oversaw a 1-1 draw vs AFC Wimbledon in his first game as interim boss in the week.

The Owls would have hoped to secure all three points but had a makeshift centre-back pairing of recalled prospect Ernie Weaver and Max Lowe – a left-back. The result leaves them 9th in the table, two points away from the League One play-off spots.

Photo by Timmy96, via Wikimedia Commons, released under CC0 1.0.

Blackpool make the trip to Hillsborough sitting in a lowly 20th, one point and one position above the drop. An encouraging start feels like a distant memory for the Tangerines, who have lost four of their last five league games.

What’s the latest Sheffield Wednesday team news?

As touched on before, Sheffield Wednesday had to deploy Weaver and Lowe at the heart of defence to deal with their centre-back shortage. Sil Swinkels is working his way back to full fitness and could only appear off the bench as Weaver cramped up.

Speaking to press, interim boss Hamshaw confirmed on Friday that all of Ricardo Santos, Di’Shon Bernard and Liam Cooper have progressed. The latter’s omission was more to give him a rest, while Santos has had a groin problem and Bernard’s minutes are being managed following his return from a long-term absence.

Joe Lumley suffered a rib injury earlier this month and was ruled out for a couple of weeks. There was no update on his fitness from Hamshaw.

Further up the pitch, summer signing Louie Barry is back in training and will be in line for a return soon. Hamshaw insisted he will not be rushed back, but the forward will be a welcome boost to the matchday squad when he is available.

Jamal Lowe serves the final match of his three-game suspension for a red card against Notts County.

Predicted Sheffield Wednesday XI to face Blackpool

(4-2-3-1)

Maynard-Brewer; Valery, Weaver, Cooper, Lowe; Mitchell, Bannan; Morrison, Slattery, Keillor-Dunn; Burstow

Hamshaw has some room to tinker with his Sheffield Wednesday side, and there could be two changes in his backline.

Maynard-Brewer continues in goal, and Weaver retains his place in defence as it would be wise to ease both Bernard and Santos back into starting football. However, after a midweek rest, the vastly experienced Cooper can start in the middle, allowing Lowe to move to the left as Reece James drops to the bench.

The midfield stays the same with Billy Mitchell and Barry Bannan starting in the deeper roles as Callum Slattery more advanced. Davis Keillor-Dunn continues on the left, but Kieran Morrison replaces Harry Gray on the right as he looks to add to his tally of three assists in 11 League One games.

Mason Burstow’s goal against AFC Wimbledon means he gets the nod again in the absence of top scorer Jamal Lowe.
