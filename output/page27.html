Jordon Hudson Already Dating After Silent Split From Bill Belichick: Report

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Jordon Hudson is reportedly already dating following her silent split from former New England Patriots head coach Bill Belichick.

According to a Friday, October 30, report from TMZ citing people close to the former beauty queen, Hudson, 25, is already “seeing other people” after her breakup with Belichick, 74, last year.

The insiders did not know Hudson’s current relationship status now that she has reportedly reentered the dating pool. Neither the current UNC football coach nor the adult cheerleader have publicly commented on the status of their often scrutinized age-gap relationship at the time of publication, though sources told TMZ the former couple remain “committed to their business partnerships and remain close friends.”

Us has reached out to reps for Belichick and Hudson for comment but did not immediately hear back.

Bill Belichick and Jordon Hudson's Silent Split Surprised UNC Staff: Report

Multiple outlets, including TMZ and People, reported earlier on Friday that the pair had privately parted ways last year. Hudson has even been a fixture at UNC home games and Belichick’s scouting trips this fall in the wake of their silent breakup.

Two staffers inside the UNC football office anonymously confirmed to USA Today Sports on Friday that they were “not aware” the pair had privately called it quits. (Us has reached out to UNC for comment.)

Bill Belichick and Jordon Hudson on the red carpet before Super Bowl LIX NFL Honors at Saenger Theatre. USA TODAY Sports via Reuters Connect

The pair met on a flight in 2021 after the legendary NFL coach signed one of Hudson’s textbooks.

After their chance encounter turned romantic, the couple often had to defend not only their 49-year age difference but Hudson’s close ties to her then-boyfriend’s career.

“Let’s take a deeper dive into the hot topic of the day: LOVE,” Hudson began a February 2025 post via Instagram. “‘What constitutes love?’ or, ‘What makes someone worthy of loving?’ are questions that require a deep analysis and could possibly enable one to understand the romances that they may not understand nor relate to.”

She continued at the time, “Love does not look the same every time you see it. People and relationships, like everything else, are impermanent; even though love is eternal. Love is not to be judged. It is never OK to harass or abuse a person based upon who they love.”

Bill Belichick Says Jordon Hudson Has Nothing 'To Do With UNC Football'

Belichick later told the Wall Street Journal that his then-girlfriend was merely more of a day-to-day help than a person deeply integrated into his professional life.

“When I took the job [at UNC] it was December, and at that time I was doing five TV shows, the radio show, I had a book coming out,” Belichick said in a September interview. “I had a lot of other things going on when I took the job. I sent an email when I got here and I said, ‘Can you please forward any requests to Jordon about my personal appearances so that I can work and coordinate them outside the university?’”

He added, “Didn’t have anything to do with the university. Well, that was leaked [and] released as ‘Jordon’s involved in every single thing that I do and she’s copied on every email.’ That’s just a flat-out lie.”
