Tether-Backed FASE Foundation Targets Scams and Stablecoin Aid

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

TL;DR: Named founding stakeholders include Anchorage Digital, Bybit, Chainalysis, Payward, Opera and the Solana Foundation; the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime is a strategic adviser. The education and aid figures are targets, not verified beneficiaries.

On October 28 Tether announced the Swiss not-for-profit Financial Access and Systemic Empowerment Foundation, bringing together businesses and non-profit partners around financial safety, inclusion, access to essential resources and responsible artificial intelligence.

The announcement and the numbers

The most concrete starting point is the dated disclosure from FASE Foundation. It offers a specific basis for assessing Tether-backed FASE Foundation’s anti-scam and aid plans without turning the story into unsupported price speculation.

Named founding stakeholders include Anchorage Digital, Bybit, Chainalysis, Payward, Opera and the Solana Foundation; the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime is a strategic adviser. FASE said an education initiative could reach up to 10 million young people and an aid pilot could potentially serve up to 1 million people.

There is a practical market question underneath the announcement: how quickly can crypto fraud prevention and humanitarian stablecoin pilots change real activity, rather than merely expand a list of proposed services? For investors, the distinction matters because product news and actual changes to liquidity or demand are not interchangeable.

Why the development matters

The question for market participants is what Tether-backed FASE Foundation’s anti-scam and aid plans establishes today and what remains uncertain. An immediate reaction is not the same as evidence of a durable economic outcome.

The initiative is a response to a persistent tension in digital assets: the same global connectivity that makes low-friction payments appealing can also make fraud more scalable. Programs that reach vulnerable populations need outcomes beyond transaction counts.

Custodial exchanges and digital-asset service providers operate several overlapping systems: wallet signing, policy engines, infrastructure permissions, customer ledgers and incident response. A weakness in one layer can matter even when a private key is not publicly exposed. The security story therefore cannot be reduced to a single cryptographic question.

From a market perspective, the first task is to separate the scale of the announcement from the scale of economic activity that has actually occurred. A company may report a large addressable market, an institutional name or a future capability, but those are not equivalent to settled transactions or recurring fee income. This is why the primary disclosure is a better starting point than a trading narrative attached to it.

Risks the headline does not resolve

None of this removes the need for independent assessment. The published evidence identifies a development, while its risk and long-term economics are still open to examination.

Stablecoin aid programs can move a digital claim across borders, but delivering useful support still requires safe wallets, local merchant acceptance, cash-out access, identity protections and appropriate oversight. Anti-scam education likewise depends on accessible language and credible community outreach.

The education and aid figures are targets, not verified beneficiaries. The foundation’s stated ambitions should be distinguished from funded commitments, completed pilots and independent measurements of impact.

Users face two different forms of risk during a security incident. Asset losses are the direct financial concern, while withdrawals, service interruptions and unclear communications can make it difficult to assess or manage exposure. Investigations are strongest when confirmed technical evidence is separated from hypotheses about attackers.

The milestones to watch

The immediate checklist is relatively straightforward: look for verifiable implementation details and avoid interpreting a stated target as an outcome that has already occurred.

The practical follow-up is whether FASE publishes measurable outcomes, safeguards for recipient data and evidence that resources reach people who need them. Its ability to coordinate commercial partners and humanitarian organizations will determine the significance of the announcement.

Investors also need to separate changes in infrastructure from changes in token demand. Revenue may accrue to a company, a protocol treasury or a service provider without necessarily creating a one-for-one benefit for holders of a related token. Market impact depends on adoption, competition and the design of whatever fees or incentives are involved.

A durable signal would be consistent follow-through: actual usage, transparent reporting and a credible explanation of risk. Short-term token-price moves can be driven by macroeconomic conditions or leverage and may say little about whether the product or initiative succeeds over months and years.

Chivu: ‘Inter forced into Milan changes, but still had wrong attitude for a derby’

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Cristian Chivu reveals he was ‘forced’ to make his unusual choices by a flu bug sweeping through the Inter squad, but crumbling so early with Milan showed ‘the wrong approach.’

The former defender has a truly negative record in the Derby della Madonnina, losing all three of his attempts as coach against the Rossoneri.

At least his team managed to score this time with Alessandro Bastoni on a Hakan Calhanoglu corner, but they were 2-0 down within 10 minutes to Christian Pulisic and Adrien Rabiot goals.

Diego Moreira had already provided two assists when he curled a free kick over the wall and the 3-1 result stayed intact after half-time, despite Inter having largely futile possession.

Chivu reveals Inter problems

VENICE, ITALY – OCTOBER 28: Head coach of FC Internazionale Cristian Chivu looks on during the Serie A match between Venezia FC and FC Internazionale at Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo on October 28, 2026 in Venice, Italy. (Photo by Maurizio Lagana/Getty Images)

“It hurts, it hurts to lose a derby where both we and everyone else had different expectations, for what the game represents and what we need to defend,” Chivu told Sky Sport Italia.

“Today we had to improvise considering the situations we had to deal with, which emerged in the last couple of days. We tried to get someone who was ready to grit their teeth, but it was tough.

“We had the wrong approach, then it wasn’t all a waste, because we scored for 2-1 and had the opportunity to draw level at 2-2 if not for that Maignan save. Then we gifted another goal just before half-time.

“It’s disappointing, we expected to do more than we did, we need to improve our approach and determination, which at times is lacking, and we need to turn the page because there’s another important game coming in in three days.”

ITALY – OCTOBER 31: Djed Spence of FC Internazionale in action during the Serie A match between AC Milan and FC Internazionale at Stadio Giuseppe Meazza on October 31, 2026 in Italy. (Photo by Antonino Lagana/Getty Images)

Inevitably, the first questions were about Chivu’s choices, because he pulled some big surprises with his starting XI.

It was a 3-4-2-1 formation with Petar Sucic and Andy Diouf in new more advanced roles behind lone centre-forward Marcus Thuram.

Even in the absence of injured captain Lautaro Martinez, he stuck to this one-striker system and didn’t make significant changes until the final minutes of the match.

“We were forced to change, because Pio Esposito has not been well, Bonny had a fever until 4.30pm and was vomiting all night, so we had to improvise,” revealed Chivu.

ITALY – OCTOBER 31: Andy Diouf of FC Internazionale in action during the Serie A match between AC Milan and FC Internazionale at Stadio Giuseppe Meazza on October 31, 2026 in Italy. (Photo by Antonino Lagana/Getty Images)

The flu bug was sweeping through the squad, as goalkeeper Ivan Provedel wasn’t even on the bench after coming down with symptoms too.

“We were also convinced it was the right choice, because two players between the lines with two attacking wingers and Andy Diouf attacking the space, we thought it was the best solution at our disposal.

“Unfortunately, it didn’t go well, but we also had some good things, we caused Milan a lot of problems once we stepped up a gear. We too have to take the blame for the initial attitude, for our sluggishness when defending, we’ve got to rediscover the determination we had last season.”

This is the eighth time in the last 11 competitive matches that Inter have gone behind, but the last two games with Fiorentina and Venezia were far from impressive performances, secured with late goals and two own goals.

ITALY – OCTOBER 31: Alessandro Bastoni of FC Internazionale Milano scores his team’s first goal during the Serie A match between AC Milan and FC Internazionale at Stadio Giuseppe Meazza on October 31, 2026 in Italy. (Photo by Marco Luzzani/Getty Images)

“It’s not easy to play every three days with the same hunger once you won the previous season. We must do things with more intensity, determination and that starts with the right initial approach,” confessed Chivu.

“We are well aware that at times we reflect on past glories and what we built, so we need to rediscover that hunger and determination, especially when taking some things for granted. We’ve got to raise the level of concentration, then after that find consistency in everything that we do.”

This was the first Serie A defeat of the season for Inter, who now risk being left behind Lazio, who host Cagliari on Sunday.

Luka Modric feels that Milan put in their best performance of the season when they defeated Inter 3-1 in the Derby della Madonnina on Saturday evening, thanking the supporters for providing an atmosphere that ‘helps you win even more’. 

Goals from Christian Pulisic and Adrien Rabiot sent Milan into a two-goal lead after 10 minutes, but their deficit was halved by an Alessandro Bastoni header midway through the first half. Diego Moreira, who had set up the first two Milan goals, restored a two-goal cushion with an excellently-struck free-kick towards the end of the first half.

Modric speaks after Milan 3-1 Inter

ITALY – OCTOBER 31: Luka Modric of AC Milan is challenged by Carlos Augusto of FC Internazionale during the Serie A match between AC Milan and FC Internazionale at Stadio Giuseppe Meazza on October 31, 2026 in Italy. (Photo by Marco Luzzani/Getty Images)

Speaking to CBS Sports Golazo after full-time on Saturday, Modric described Milan’s derby victory as: “An amazing win, an amazing performance. 

“From the first minute, I think this is maybe our best game of the season. Overall, we deserved the victory and we’re really happy after a disappointing result midweek against Bologna.”

Modric was asked how he helped Milan and especially their younger players bounce back after a disappointing defeat against Bologna earlier this week.

And asked for a comment on the atmosphere inside San Siro on Saturday, Modric said: “It was amazing. Ever since I came here, the atmosphere’s been incredible, every game but especially in these kinds of games, derby games. Tonight, from the warm-up, they were incredible and this type of atmosphere helps you win even more.”

Premier League boss challenges board to sack him amid deeper problems at club

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Oliver Glasner has insisted that a new manager at Nottingham Forest will not guarantee improvement.

Forest were beaten 2-0 by Brentford on Saturday and have fallen into the Premier League relegation zone with nine games played.

Glasner believes he is safe at Forest and says that a change of manager would not bring improvement to the clubGetty

They also have just one win from ten in all competitions since Glasner joined as head coach in July.

The former Crystal Palace boss is the fifth name to take the job in less than three years after Steve Cooper was sacked in December 2023.

Nuno Espirito Santo, Ange Postecoglou, Sean Dyche and Vitor Pereira preceded Glasner, who was very transparent on the club’s struggles.

Speaking to the media after the loss at Brentford, he was asked if he has the freedom to make the changes he wants at Forest.

Glasner insists Forest sack won’t bring change

“There is no other way,” he declared. “And if I don’t have the freedom, they have to sack me. That’s it.”

And when speaking to talkSPORT’s Jack Cunningham, Glasner explained how Forest’s problems run deeper than whoever is in charge.

“The reality is we lost four of our last five games, and that means for me it’s time also to speak very honestly about it. It’s time to change things because otherwise things will happen again

“We have to change things, I have to change a few things and I will definitely change a few things.”

Glasner added: “I feel now maybe I was a little bit too patient and made a little bit too many compromises on a few things. And this is what I will definitely change.

“Then it’s up to the players if they buy in, I think then we can get the turnaround and if not, the sixth manager will follow.”

Brentford scored a goal in each half against Forest to inflict a fifth league defeat on their opponentsGetty

On whether he is feeling the pressure from Forest owner Evangelos Marinakis, Glasner stated: “No, I don’t have pressure, I think nobody needs to have pity with the manager.

“You get sacked, you go home with your family and, if you want, you can start a job within two weeks. But the players, the staff, they have to work and continue in the same situation like last year.

“If there have been four managers and things didn’t really change and now it’s the fifth one and things don’t really change, I think it’s deeper than changing the manager.

“But again, I have no concerns at all about my position.

“I came to Nottingham Forest to help the club establish in the top half of the Premier League, helping the owner to get his vision to reality, and for this we have to change things 100 percent, otherwise it’s impossible.”

Having his final say on the result to talkSPORT, Glasner remarked: “We want to be successful, but again, we need to change a few things.

Glasner has lost six of his ten matches across all competitions at Forest, and won just one of thoseGetty

“And let’s say maybe the most obvious one is we are outrun by the opposition in every single game, this is number one. We’re losing duels, I think today it was 61 per cent for Brentford in ground duels.

“That’s the foundation, the basics of everything. Then we can talk about tactics, how we’re playing looks nice. But in the important areas, you need to win the duels.”

Forest are 19th and joined by Palace and Sunderland in the relegation zone, although Manchester United are only a point above them after their 2-0 loss to Chelsea.

They host leaders Manchester City at the City Ground next Saturday before the November international break.
