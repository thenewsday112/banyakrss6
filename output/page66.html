Who are Ilhan Omar’s lawyers? Meet the high-powered Dem attorneys the embattled rep hired

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Rep. Ilhan Omar, D-Minn., has tapped two high-powered Washington, D.C.-based attorneys who have previously represented elected officials at the highest echelon of American politics.

Amid dueling federal investigations — one for immigration fraud and another for financial disclosure discrepancies — the embattled Somali-born lawmaker hired Amy Jeffress and Joshua Matz, according to a CBS report. Both are partners at Hecker Fink LLP.

The attorneys have in the past represented Democrat clients and taken on left-leaning cases especially those taking aim at President Donald Trump, his administration and his supporters.

Jeffress is a former federal prosecutor whose biography says she tried more than 40 cases during a decade in the U.S. Attorney’s Office in the District of Columbia, where she was chief of the National Security Section. She has also been a Department of Justice (DOJ) attaché.

She served as former President Joe Biden's attorney in his lawsuit against the DOJ earlier this year. In that case, Biden sought to halt the release of audio recordings between himself and his "Promise Me, Dad" book ghostwriter, Mark Zwonitzer. 

The audio was recorded in 2016 and 2017 and was subject to a FOIA request from the Heritage Foundation in 2024.

It was cited during special counsel Robert Hur's investigation into whether Biden illegally retained classified information from his term as vice president and piqued the interest of conservatives demanding answers about Biden's mental acuity.

The court declined to block the release of the recordings, in which Biden had frequent memory lapses, and they were made public in July.

Before her time at Hecker Fink, Jeffress, a 1992 Yale Law graduate, served as counsel for then-House Speaker Nancy Pelosi and the Democrat-led House January 6 committee, which spent two years investigating the riot at the U.S. Capitol on Jan. 6, 2021. She represented the committee when it was sued by a Trump campaign spokesperson, Taylor Budowich, after the committee subpoenaed his bank records.

Jeffress also represented Trump foe and former FBI attorney Lisa Page, a key figure in the left's Russiagate conspiracy theory, during congressional scrutiny into the FBI's handling of the matter. Page later sued the DOJ for releasing her text messages with her FBI colleague and lover at the time, Peter Strzok.

In the messages, Strzok assuaged Page's concerns that Trump would become president by vowing that the FBI would stop it and referred to an "insurance policy" against his presidency. That case was settled in 2024.

Jeffress is married to U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper of the District of Washington, D.C., who in May ruled that it was illegal for the John F. Kennedy Performing Arts Center to add Trump's name. Trump publicly criticized Cooper as "highly conflicted" due to his relationship with Jeffress.

Matz is also a partner at Hecker Fink. He graduated from Harvard Law and clerked for former Supreme Court Justice Anthony Kennedy.

"Joshua’s practice has long included a focus on democracy and elections," his biography page says.

He represented the state of Pennsylvania in defending the certification of its election results, including arguing before the Supreme Court.

He was counsel to House Democrats during both failed impeachment attempts against Trump during his first term in office.

Matz also represented E. Jean Carroll in two separate civil cases against Trump related to his denial of her sexual assault claims against him. He was never criminally charged. In 2022, Carroll accused Trump of defaming her in his denial of her account of an incident she claims happened in 1996. A New York jury found Trump liable for defamation and for sexual assault.

Prior to that case, in 2019, a jury awarded Carroll $83.3 million for defamation stemming from other comments made by Trump.

Matz represented students, parents and teachers who opposed Florida's Parental Rights in Education Act, which his biography describes as the "Don't Say Gay" law, which restricted teaching students about sexual orientation and gender identity.

He also successfully represented Democratic Kentucky Gov. Andy Beshear when he was sued for COVID-19 lockdowns, including school closures, and victims of the 2017 "Unite the Right" rally in Charlottesville.

Now, Matz and Jeffress are representing Omar as she seeks to battle two federal probes. In a video message posted to her social media earlier this month, the lawmaker pleaded with her supporters to keep sending money, noting that her fight against the investigations would be costly.

Omar's attorneys did not respond to Fox News Digital's request for comment. Neither did the White House.

Jamie Carragher has issued an apology for his ‘clumsy language’ that prompted Aston Villa to threaten legal action against the Liverpool legend.

The former defender this week suggested Villa boss Unai Emery would be interested in taking the reins at Manchester United should Michael Carrick depart Old Trafford.

Carrick’s position has come under significant threat this season with Emery among United’s list of managerial targets should a change be made in the dugout.

The topic was discussed on this week’s ‘Stick to Football’ with Carragher praising the work Emery has done during his four years at Villa Park.

Chevy’s 2027 Silverado Police Truck Is Built for 112-MPH Pursuits

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Chevrolet is continuing its strategic push into the law enforcement sector with a first look at the 2027 Chevy Silverado Police Pursuit Vehicle (PPV). As police departments across the country increasingly phase out traditional sedans in favor of larger, more capable trucks, this pursuit-rated pickup is heavily positioned as a versatile fleet option. Building directly on the successful foundation established when the 2023 Chevy Silverado PPV was revealed, the 2027 iteration introduces updated front-end styling and heavy-duty interior features specifically engineered to accommodate bulky utility belts and complex radio setups.

The broad transition toward full-size trucks for daily patrol duty allows departments to utilize vehicles fully capable of clearing curbs, safely transporting hundreds of pounds of tactical gear, and withstanding severe daily wear and tear. Equipped with heavy-duty steel wheels, massive 16-inch Brembo brakes, and a standard locking rear differential, the 2027 Silverado PPV is expressly built for demanding municipal use.

Emergency Vehicles

V8 Power and Proven Reliability

While several competing automakers are actively introducing electric options to municipal fleets, Chevrolet is maintaining a strict focus on traditional internal combustion for highway patrol units that require sustained power and rapid refueling capabilities. Specs from a report from GM Authority show that the 2027 Silverado PPV is powered by a robust 5.3-liter V8 engine producing a reliable 355 horsepower. This proven powerplant is mated to a heavy-duty 10-speed automatic transmission and a high-capacity alternator necessary for continuously running emergency lighting and communication equipment over grueling shifts.

Fleet managers heavily prioritize proven, reliable platforms to minimize vehicle downtime. Following highly publicized reports that the some Police Squads are having bad luck with their Dodge Durangos due to unexpected mechanical issues, proven powertrain options remain a major selling point for Chevrolet’s commercial and heavy-duty truck division.

Chevrolet

Pursuit-Rated Performance

To meet strict law enforcement standards, the Silverado PPV utilizes a specially tuned suspension system that noticeably lowers the truck’s ride height for substantially improved high-speed stability. It is officially pursuit-rated by the manufacturer to reach top speeds of 112 mph, having undergone rigorous independent testing to ensure it can safely handle aggressive cornering and extreme braking without excessive brake fade.

This dedicated factory tuning is absolutely essential for officer safety and departmental liability management. In sharp contrast to standard civilian performance models occasionally pressed into service, like this California Camaro cop car that is banned from sport and track modes due to restrictive departmental policy, the Silverado PPV is explicitly engineered and fully warrantied to operate safely at its mechanical limits during active pursuits. By effectively balancing heavy-duty utility with pursuit-rated high-speed stability, the 2027 Silverado PPV provides modern departments with a highly capable, purpose-built patrol option.

Jamie Carragher apologises for &#8216;clumsy and wrong&#8217; Aston Villa comments after legal threat

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Jamie Carragher has apologised to Aston Villa after the club launched legal action over comments made by the pundit.

The Liverpool legend spoke on The Overlap earlier in the week about whether or not Manchester United would hire Villa boss Unai Emery.

Villa have announced they will take legal action against CarragherGetty

“Aston Villa will never be Manchester United,” he said, before looking at Emery’s past spells at Arsenal and Paris Saint-Germain and adding: “Surely he would want that opportunity to manage a giant again.

“I’m not trying to annoy Villa fans, but the reason I’m saying this is there is a chain in football, whether you like it or not.

“Real Madrid will take Man United’s best player, they took (Cristiano) Ronaldo. There’s very few above United, the same with Liverpool.

“But there’ll be teams below Aston Villa, they’ll take their manager or they’ll take their players. So, shut the f*** up Villa fans!”

What did Aston Villa say in their statement?

Villa announced their intentions to begin legal matters, as they took to social media on Friday night to address Carragher’s comments.

A statement read: “Aston Villa will initiate legal action against Jamie Carragher for his unacceptable insults about the Club and its fans.

“Aston Villa will not allow anyone to stop our ascension in English and European football. Nassef Sawiris, Chairman.”

Carragher apologises

The 48-year-old took to social media on Saturday with his own statement on the situation – and decided he was in the wrong.

Carragher said: “I’d like to apologise to Aston Villa, the owners & their fans.

“My language this week was clumsy & wrong.

Emery has been linked with the United job amid his success with Villa since joining in October 2022Getty

“I can assure Villa fans it was tongue in cheek but I can understand it’s not come across that way, so apologies.

“Good luck today UTV!”

Emery linked with Old Trafford hotseat

United head coach Michael Carrick has faced increased pressure over recent weeks having not won any of their last eight matches.

Amid a potential managerial change, Emery has been a speculated option to take over.

The 54-year-old has been successful in getting Villa back into the Champions League and won last season’s Europa League.

However, whilst Emery did consider leaving in the summer, talkSPORT has been told that he is happy at Villa Park.

Man United have won one Premier League game all seasonGetty

Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann are also understood to be names in consideration at United should they sack Carrick.

The ex-midfielder has been given time to turn things around despite their poor run of form.

Carrick will be in charge of the team when they take on Chelsea at Saturday lunchtime, live on talkSPORT.

Anything but a win could spell the end of his tenure at United – just five months after taking the job permanently.

United are 16th in the Premier League on seven points heading into the fixture.
