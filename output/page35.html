Bitcoin turns 18 today. Its founder is still missing, his presumed fortune is worth $85 billion, and AI is beginning to reconstruct how it all started.

On October 31, 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto released the Bitcoin whitepaper, proposing money that could move without a bank. Today, Bitcoin trades near $77,330 and has a market value of about $1.55 trillion.

“I’ve been working on a new electronic cash system that’s fully peer-to-peer, with no trusted third party,” Satoshi wrote at the time.

The breakthrough was simple to explain. Computers across a network would verify transactions and prevent the same Bitcoin from being spent twice. Satoshi stopped posting publicly in 2010. Bitcoin continued without him.

Bitcoin’s Satoshi Paradox

The $85 Billion Shadow Over Bitcoin

Researchers attribute roughly 1.1 million BTC to an early miner widely believed to be Satoshi. Most of those coins remain untouched. At current prices, the estimated fortune is worth around $85 billion.

Nobody knows who controls the private keys today.

If those coins suddenly moved, traders could fear that Satoshi had returned, or that someone had gained access to the wallets. A transfer alone would prove no sale. The uncertainty could still shake the market.

AI Is Digging Into Bitcoin’s Past

This month, developer T4T5 used Anthropic’s Claude to recreate preserved Bitcoin software from 2009, matching the original file byte for byte. The actual launch-day program remains missing.

Separately, Claude traced mysterious numbers in NSA-supplied encryption standards to a timestamp-based random generator. That reduced long-standing suspicions of hidden weaknesses. Neither discovery exposed a flaw in Bitcoin.

Still, billionaire investor Chamath Palihapitiya recently warned that Bitcoin could eventually be hacked and “go to zero.”

Miranda Lambert and Blake Shelton's Candid Comments About Their Divorce

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Blake Shelton and Miranda Lambert shocked country music fans in July 2015 when they announced the end of their four-year marriage.

“This is not the future we envisioned, and it is with heavy hearts that we move forward separately. We are real people, with real lives, with real families, friends and colleagues,” Shelton and Lambert told Us in a joint statement. “Therefore, we kindly ask for privacy and compassion concerning this very personal matter.”

Days later, Lambert addressed the breakup during a Nashville show. “Well, I’ve been living off of caffeine and sad songs lately,” she told the crowd. “So, we’re gonna drink some caffeine and sing sad songs.”

In the years since, both artists have opened up — in interviews, on stage and through their music — about heartbreak, healing and what came next. (Shelton and Lambert are now married to Gwen Stefani and Brendan McLaughlin, respectively.)

Miranda Lambert's Ups and Downs: Blake Divorce, Fan Selfie Drama, More

Keep scrolling to read their candid comments about their divorce:

The Split Happened ‘So Fast’

Appearing on the Bobby Bones Show in September 2015, Shelton explained how quickly the divorce was finalized.

“In Oklahoma, it happens so fast,” he said. “Like, Miranda and I didn’t have any kids, and we had a prenuptial agreement, whatever we had. Once we filed for divorce, it was over in, like, I think it was like a nine or 10-day waiting period until it’s over.”

He added that the two agreed to stay civil, noting, “Our whole thing was, we are going to be cool about this, it is what it is. We’re buddies.”

Blake Shelton Went on a ‘Divorce Diet’

Fans quickly noticed Shelton’s weight loss after the breakup, and he had a simple explanation.

“Well, you know, you should try the divorce diet,” he quipped to host Blair Garner on America’s Morning Show in September 2015. “The whole stress of getting through all of that is just…It was just weird. I’m not eating as many fried pickles and stuff. I’m doing great.”

Blake Shelton Is ‘In a Good Place’ 1 Month Later

Weeks later, Shelton told the CMT Hot 20 Countdown show he was finding his footing. “I’m good, I’m good,” he said. “I’m in a good place. I wasn’t, but I am now. It’s amazing how quickly life can turn around for you.”

On Late Night With Seth Meyers later that month, Shelton predicted some uncomfortable moments at country music’s biggest night. “This year’s gonna be awkward at the CMAs,” he joked. “[Host] Brad Paisley is not doing his job if he doesn’t look down there and go ‘Ha ha!’ You have to address the elephant in the room.”

Miranda Lambert Says They Gave Their Marriage the ‘Best College Try’

Lambert told Cosmopolitan in November 2015 she had no regrets about the marriage.

“Marriage is a tough business, and we gave it our best college try,” she said. “I had a great relationship with an amazing man. I know what good is. I have a great launching pad for the future. I will never take that for granted.”

She also said there were no teams to pick, adding, “We don’t need all the opinions. We just need y’all to be supportive, and nobody pick a side — because there ain’t a side.”

Miranda Lambert Won’t Take Pain ‘for Granted’

Speaking to Billboard in July 2017 about her song “The Weight of These Wings,” Lambert said the split changed her.

“I won’t look at things ever again in the same light, because different things in your life bring you to a deeper level with yourself,” Lambert acknowledged. “I won’t take pain for granted anymore.”

Blake Shelton Calls Gwen Stefani a ‘Miracle’ After Divorce

Shelton credited his now-wife Stefani with pulling him out of a dark place.

“When I was going through my divorce and just, you know, hit rock bottom just like anybody does when they go through something that devastating,” he said on Sunday Today With Willie Geist in June 2018. “It’s a miracle that I met somebody that was going through the same exact thing that I was at the same exact moment in time.”

Miranda Lambert ‘Wasn’t Prepared’ for the Viral Breakup Reaction

Nearly seven years later, Lambert told CBS News the public scrutiny caught her off guard.

“I wasn’t prepared for that,” she said in 2022. “Well, I don’t think anyone is. It’s not nice sometimes, but I think you’ve gotta take it with a grain of salt. I’m a singer-songwriter so, luckily, I can tell my whole truth. I will not lie in my music.”

Miranda Lambert Dove ‘Headfirst’ Into Songwriting After the Split

Speaking on the “And the Writer Is…” podcast in October 2026, Lambert reflected on how their divorce fueled a creative outpouring.

“I got divorced and that sucks for anyone, especially in the public eye, especially on tour, especially in the middle of a very public career, two of us, you know what I mean?” she said. “It just was a really not fun time. I, in the meantime, had moved back to Nashville full-time, and so I dove headfirst into songwriting.”

Lambert channeled the heartbreak into a prolific writing period.

“When I moved back [to Nashville] in 2015, I gathered up all of my friends and relationships from, you know, all of the previous years and just used it, used the misery,” she said. “I used the staying out too late at honky-tonks. I used what I was living and my friends went on that journey with me, friends being songwriters, and I think I wrote 75 songs in, like, a year. I mean, in, like, the first four months I moved back.”

With 88 yards on Sunday, Derrick Henry will jump to No. 7 all-time in rushing yards

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Ravens running back Derrick Henry, who turns 33 in early January, continues to get it done at a high level. A solid performance on Sunday at Buffalo will put him ahead of a pair of Hall of Famers on the all-time rushing list.

Henry enters the Week 8 game with 13,597 career rushing yards. With only 66 yards, he'll pass Jerome Bettis for No. 8 on the career list. With 88, Henry will leapfrog LaDainian Tomlinson.

If, along the way, Henry scores a rushing touchdown, he'll join Hall of Famers Marshall Faulk and Marcus Allen as the only players with 40 or more rushing touchdowns with multiple teams.

Faulk did it with the Colts and the Rams. Allen did it with the Raiders and the Chiefs. Henry has previously gotten there with the Titans.

The overriding question for Henry is how high he can go on the career rushing list. The current record holder, Hall of Famer Emmitt Smith, believes Henry can catch and beat Smith's all-time record of 18,355 yards.

Henry needs 4,758 yards to match Smith. To get there, Henry probably will have to finish 2026 strong and keep it going for four more years.

By then, it's possible that Henry will be playing for a third team. It would be fitting if the Cowboys — who weren't interested when Henry became a free agent — would be the team with which Henry breaks Smith's record.

Dodge Viper's Old Factory Is Now Home to a Car Design Podcast

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The Science of Looking Good

Car designs do more than make vehicles look good. They help establish a brand’s identity and can even attract buyers. Stellantis wants to shed light on automotive design with a new podcast in which Chief Design Officer Ralph Gilles will explore the design and creative processes behind vehicles through conversations with designers, engineers, executives, and creators.

Gilles joined the company as a designer in 1992 and has held various positions since, including president and CEO of the Dodge brand. He notably led the design of the Chrysler 300, which gave the automaker a luxurious-looking sedan and earned it the nickname “poor man’s Bentley.” The podcast is recorded at the former Conner Avenue Assembly Plant in Detroit, where Dodge previously manufactured the Viper, another iconic design known for its low-slung silhouette and long hood housing a large-displacement V10 engine.

Stellantis

Turning Sketches Into Stories

Dubbed “Car Concepts with Ralph Gilles,” the podcast aims to cater to different audiences, including car enthusiasts, students, design enthusiasts, and business leaders. Based on the press release, the podcast is intended to explore the human side of automotive development rather than serve as a platform for announcing new models.

For the first season, the guest list includes former Chrysler executive Bob Lutz, automotive personality Cristy Lee, content creator Garrett Reed, and Stellantis designers Scott Krugger and Gilles Vidal. The latter recently presented six concept cars ahead of the Paris Motor Show, including the quirky-looking Fiat Multiplay, which combines elements of a pickup, SUV, and MPV.

Retired Mercedes-Benz design chief Gorden Wagener will also be among the guests, potentially offering insights into luxury-car design, which involves different priorities than designing mass-market vehicles. His participation is particularly interesting given Chrysler’s traditional focus on mainstream buyers, while Maserati serves as Stellantis’ primary luxury brand.

Chrysler

The Weekly Design Pit Stop

“Car Concepts with Ralph Gilles” is available in video format on the Stellantis North America YouTube channel, with heritage and new vehicles providing the backdrop for each conversation. Audio versions are also available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, YouTube, and Audible. The nine-episode first season kicked off on October 29, with new episodes released every Thursday.

For reference, Stellantis owns 14 automotive brands, including Abarth, Alfa Romeo, Chrysler, Citroën, Dodge, DS Automobiles, Fiat, Jeep, Lancia, Maserati, Opel, Peugeot, Ram, and Vauxhall. With such a diverse portfolio, the podcast has plenty of potential topics to explore. This variety could also help attract a broader audience of automotive enthusiasts.
