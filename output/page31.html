MILIMETRIK ARCHITECTS CONVERTS RAMI LIBRARY INTO FAMILY FESTIVAL

 

Staged across the historic Rami Library in Istanbul, AileFest 2026 is a temporary spatial intervention designed by Turkish practice Milimetrik Architects. Held from September 26th to October 6th, 2026, the 91,000-sqm installation adapted the site’s historic courtyard and interior halls into an interconnected landscape for family-oriented recreation, education, and cultural programming, hosting approximately 825,000 visitors over an eleven-day period.

all images ©Fevzi Ondu

 

 

MODULAR COURTYARD INSTALLATIONS FOSTER OPEN-ENDED RECREATION

 

The project employs a child-centric, semi-structured spatial syntax designed around ergonomic scales for varying age demographics. Rather than defining rigid play equipment, the studio populated the expansive outdoor courtyard with modular geometric volumes, abstract shelters, and chromatic gathering nodes that allow for open-ended circulation and spontaneous use. Inside the library, the Book Fair departs from standard orthogonal booth layouts, organizing the space with pitched house-shaped pavilions, primary-toned modular display stands, and graphic floor patterns. The intervention establishes continuous circulation between the open-air courtyards and the historic interior galleries, utilizing temporary timber-and-steel fabrications to activate the civic site without altering its heritage fabric.

Michael Carrick is expected to be in the Manchester United dugout on Tuesday night against Roma, talkSPORT understands.

United were beaten 2-0 by Chelsea on Saturday afternoon and went down to ten man when Lisandro Martinez was sent off.

Carrick is set to be given more time as United head coach despite their winless run of nine matchesGetty

The Red Devils are highly unlikely to make a head coach change until the upcoming international break at the earliest, even though the hierarchy held summit talks.

Senior boardroom figures are still hoping Carrick can turn around the club’s worst start to a season since the 1986/87 campaign.

Carrick is preparing to be in charge for the Champions League clash at home to Roma.

United will then welcome Aston Villa to Old Trafford on November 8.

Dismal United run continues

Carrick was on course for a first United victory since September 10 as they led after half an hour through Matheus Cunha.

However, the forward was found to be in an offside position and the goal was ruled out.

Morgan Rogers waltzed through their defence and chipped over Senne Lammens at the start of the second half to put Chelsea ahead.

Martinez was then shown two yellow cards in quick succession, the second of which saw him hit Wesley Fofana in the face with his arm.

Cole Palmer scored the penalty despite the best efforts of Lammens, and Estevao Willian had a second goal disallowed for the hosts.

United missed a string of chances, particularly during the first half, and fell to their fourth league defeat as a result.

Carrick has at least two more matches in charge at United, with a tough test against Roma next upGetty

Manchester City, Brighton, Atletico Madrid, Como, Bournemouth and Chelsea have all beaten United since they beat Sabah FK last month.

United have also drawn with Fulham, Tottenham and Leeds United in their winless run of nine matches.

They only remain outside the bottom three on goal difference, with Crystal Palace, Nottingham Forest and Sunderland all below them.

Defeat to Roma during the week could see them slip down into the elimination zone of the Champions League.

Meanwhile, a similar result against Villa next weekend may see them enter the international break as a relegation-threatend side.

Binance founder Changpeng Zhao (CZ) said Saturday that SpaceX will be one of the best stocks to hold for years. Hours earlier, the shares closed at $134.72, just under their $135 June listing price.

That leaves anyone who bought at the listing slightly underwater. Wall Street analysts still see 86% upside. Options traders, however, are paying for a 14% swing in either direction by mid-December.

SpaceX Stock (SPCX) Performance Since IPO. Source: Yahoo Finance

Is Binance Selling Its SpaceX Shares?

Binance holds more than $1 billion in SpaceX stock. The stake began as a $500 million bet on Elon Musk’s 2022 Twitter takeover.

On Friday, CZ cited those shares while tallying the hack shortfall, as BeInCrypto reported. Some users read it as a threat to sell. CZ rejected that.

“I never said “selling SPCX shares”… I don’t make threats… And I think it will be one of the best stocks to hold for a long time to come,” CZ articulated.

Binance co-CEO Yi He has said this year’s revenue covers the losses from the $6 billion heist.

Why Are Options Traders Bracing for December?

Lockups bar early investors from selling for a set period after a listing. SpaceX’s final batch of locked-up shares becomes sellable on December 8, according to the schedule published on Nasdaq.

Options contracts expiring December 18 price a move of about $19, or 14%, according to OptionCharts. That implies a range of $117 to $155.

SpaceX stock expected move by options expiry, Source: OptionCharts

The last major unlock went the other way. When over 911 million insider shares unlocked on August 6, SPCX rose more than 6% toward $115.

How Much Upside Do Analysts See?

Of 35 analysts tracked by TipRanks, 29 rate SpaceX stock a Buy. Their average target of $250.34 sits about 86% above Friday’s close.

Even the lowest target, Piper Sandler’s $140, is above the current price.

SpaceX stock analyst price targets, Source: TipRanks

Valuation is the bear case. SpaceX trades at about 518 times its annual profit, per MarketBeat, even after third-quarter revenue jumped 161% to $14.57 billion.

The December 8 unlock lands 10 days before those options expire.
