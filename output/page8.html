Manchester United are reportedly considering a January move for Fulham defender Calvin Bassey as the club prepares to strengthen a squad that has been badly exposed by injuries.

The 26-year-old has been one of Fulham’s standout performers this season and has also taken on the captaincy.

His ability to operate at both centre-back and left-back makes him especially attractive to United, who are dealing with problems in both areas.

Man United are interested in Calvin Bassey

According to TEAMtalk, United’s recruitment department has been impressed by Bassey and is monitoring his situation closely.

The Nigeria international’s contract expires next summer, although Fulham hold an option to extend it by another year. Newcastle, Everton, Inter Milan, Borussia Dortmund and RB Leipzig are also understood to be following him.

Bassey’s versatility is a major reason for United’s interest. He is primarily a centre-back but can also cover on the left, which is significant given Luke Shaw and Patrick Dorgu are currently unavailable.

Bassey is viewed as a possible solution to United’s defensive issues, particularly with Lisandro Martinez’s future uncertain.

The need for January reinforcements goes beyond defence. The Times reports that United are ready to make at least one signing after injuries exposed the lack of depth at left-back and centre-forward.

Benjamin Šeško is expected to be sidelined until at least January, leaving Bryan Mbeumo to fill in through the middle.

Bassey could solve two problems at once

Calvin Bassey in action for Fulham (Photo by Tom Dulat/Getty Images)

Bassey makes sense because United need players who can cover multiple positions rather than another specialist who only fixes one issue.

His Premier League experience also reduces the adaptation risk, while his contract situation could make him more attainable than several younger defensive targets.

The bigger question is whether United should prioritise him over a natural left-back.

If Shaw and Dorgu remain injury concerns, signing someone who can play both centrally and wide would give the squad much-needed flexibility. That makes Bassey particularly appealing.

United still need a striker as well, so January spending will have to be carefully managed.

For that reason, a versatile defender such as Bassey could be one of the most logical deals available: experienced, physically suited to the league and capable of covering two positions in a squad that badly needs depth.

Arsenal are continuing to track Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi, but they may need to pay around €50million to bring one of Europe’s fastest-rising young forwards to the Emirates.

The 22-year-old has enjoyed an outstanding start to the season and is now firmly on Arsenal’s radar as Mikel Arteta assesses how to strengthen his attack ahead of 2027.

Tresoldi’s goalscoring form has also attracted interest from several other major clubs, meaning the Gunners could face serious competition if they decide to move.

Football Insider reveals Club Brugge valuation

According to Football Insider, Arsenal are aware that Tresoldi’s asking price is rising rapidly and that Club Brugge currently value him at around £42million, roughly €50million.

The striker has scored 12 goals in 14 appearances in all competitions this season and has already delivered against elite opposition, including a Champions League hat-trick away to Inter Milan.

Football Insider also reports that Arsenal have no major concerns about paying a significant fee if they remain convinced by his development.

Sky Germany has separately confirmed that Arsenal are maintaining strong interest, although a January transfer is considered unlikely. Tresoldi is contracted until 2029 and Club Brugge would prefer to keep him until next summer.

CaughtOffside has also reported that Arsenal remain firmly in the race, with Manchester United, Tottenham and Chelsea among the other clubs monitoring him.

€50m could look like a good value for Arsenal

Nicolo Tresoldi of Club Brugge (Photo by Dean Mouhtaropoulos/Getty Images)

From Arsenal’s perspective, Tresoldi makes a lot of sense.

He is young, mobile, technically improving and already showing he can score in the Champions League. At 22, he also fits the club’s long-term recruitment model far better than another short-term attacking solution.

The complication is timing. Club Brugge have no reason to sell in January, and Tresoldi’s value could rise significantly if he maintains his current scoring rate. Brugge may eventually demand a club-record fee. That creates a difficult decision for Arsenal.

Waiting until summer may make the deal easier structurally, but it could also mean paying considerably more and facing stronger competition.

If Arsenal genuinely believe Tresoldi can become an elite No.9, €50million may not look excessive for long. The bigger risk could actually be waiting until his price moves beyond their comfort zone.

Manchester United are reportedly considering Barcelona midfielder Fermín López as they assess potential successors to Bruno Fernandes, although convincing the Spanish champions to sell could prove extremely difficult.

The 23-year-old has enjoyed another productive start to the season, combining goals with the energy and versatility needed to operate as a No.10, central midfielder or wide attacker.

Those qualities have put him on United’s radar at a time when Fernandes’ long-term future remains uncertain.

Man United are interested in Fermín López

According to the Daily Mail, Man United have shown interest in Fermín as they explore attacking additions ahead of future transfer windows.

The Spain international is valued at around £70million, with Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain also among his admirers. Atlético Madrid midfielder Pablo Barrios is another option United are monitoring.

United have joined Chelsea in pursuing Fermín, although Barcelona’s financial situation could ultimately influence whether they would consider a major offer.

However, Fabrizio Romano has played down the possibility of an exit, describing Fermín as “absolutely untouchable” and saying Barcelona view him as crucial for both the present and future.

Romano added that the midfielder is happy at the club and recently signed a new contract.

Hansi Flick has also been full of praise for him. Speaking earlier this year, the Barcelona coach said: “He can change games, and that’s what we need.” Flick also described Fermín as “fantastic in front of goal”.

Fermín fits the profile but this deal looks difficult

Fermin Lopez warming up for Barcelona (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

Fermín makes plenty of sense as a long-term United target.

He scores goals, presses aggressively and can operate between the lines, qualities that could eventually help replace Fernandes’ creative influence.

Fernandes returned to the starting XI against Chelsea, but United still lost 2-0, extending their winless run to nine matches.

The bigger obstacle is Barcelona.

If Fermín genuinely remains untouchable, £70m may not even start a serious conversation.

United should certainly continue monitoring him, but unless the player’s position changes, this currently looks more like an ambitious long-term target than a realistic January deal.

Manchester United could be heading towards a major defensive change in 2027, with Lisandro Martínez reportedly attracting serious interest from both Barcelona and Atlético Madrid.

The Argentina international’s contract expires at the end of the season, although United retain an option to extend it by another year.

However, the relationship between player and club has deteriorated badly in recent weeks, raising the possibility of a January departure if a suitable offer arrives.

Barcelona and Atlético want Martínez

According to AS, both Barcelona and Atlético Madrid are positioning themselves to sign Martínez.

The report claims neither United nor the player currently appears keen to exercise the one-year extension option, while a winter exit is also being considered if an acceptable fee is offered.

Martínez would otherwise be free to negotiate with foreign clubs from January ahead of a summer free transfer.

The tension follows his public criticism of United’s transfer planning after the Champions League defeat to Atlético. Sky Sports reported that Martínez had already pushed for a move to Atlético during the summer, but no suitable offer arrived.

Barcelona’s interest is becoming more concrete too. The Catalan club have made enquiries about the conditions of a possible deal.

Selling Martínez in January looks sensible from Man United

Lisandro Martinez (Photo by Carl Recine/Getty Images)

United face an awkward decision.

Martínez remains one of their best defenders and, according to AS, has been the club’s strongest centre-back statistically this season, leading United defenders in clearances, recoveries and duel success.

But keeping an unhappy player into the final months of his contract carries obvious risk.

United are already planning a defensive rebuild, with new centre-backs and left-backs being considered.

If Barcelona or Atlético offer a reasonable fee in January, United may be better off selling rather than allowing the situation to drift.

The alternative is activating the extension purely to protect his value, but that could create another prolonged contract saga.

At this stage, the relationship looks increasingly close to its natural end.
