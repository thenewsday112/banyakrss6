As Eagles prepare for Sunday night, questions of effort from Monday night linger

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

As the Eagles plunged to their fifth straight loss, questions emerged regarding effort, or lack thereof.

Or, more fundamentally, whether the defensive line was even bothering to get into their stances.

To no surprise, then, effort was a theme of the week, specifically when reporters had a midweek chance to pose questions to coach Nick Sirianni.

Asked whether there was an issue with effort on Monday night against the Cowboys, and how the coaching staff gauges whether effort is an issue, Sirianni said this: "We grade every play based off of what they did, we grade every play based off of their assignment alignment and we grade every play off of effort. That's not only in games, but that's also at every practice, because if we were just grading it in games and not emphasizing it on every play in every practice, then we wouldn't be doing our job.

"I did not see an effort issue on Monday night. Obviously, it was other things, which I'm sure you guys will ask me about. There were other things, but effort in no way, shape [or] form was an issue there.

"The plays -- now, do you bat 100% on effort? No, you don't play a perfect football game. The plays, anytime there is an effort stand out in a positive way, we show it to the team and show what the standard is. Anytime there's one that we feel like there was a loaf, we point that out and show that to the team. But I didn't think that we had -- I didn't look at the tape and say, 'Hey, this was an effort issue, and this is why this play happened or that play happened.' That's how I graded the film."

He later was asked whether players get grades on a weekly basis regarding topics like assignment, alignment, and effort.

"That would be crazy not to tell them what they've done and what they haven't done," Sirianni said. "We go over that with them. We watch the game with them, and everything is discussed with these guys. I guess I'm a little confused by the question. If we want them to get better, we've got to go over all the mistakes and everything with it."

The reporters eventually attempted to address the confusion by explaining to Sirianni the perception that effort was an issue. So what does Sirianni think about the questions in that regard?

"I think in that situation, everyone's going to look to find answers in a five-game losing streak," Sirianni said. "Just because the result -- If we win that game, do they still question the effort? I don't know. I can't -- I'm not them. My job is to look at the daily process of it and the daily work and what objectively happened in that game and not react based off of the outcome. I get it. I would understand [that] everyone's looking for answers. I don't give you a lot of answers up here. I get that. I understand that. I do respect the job that you guys have to do, but I'm always going to probably keep it tight to the vest.

"But again, it's not about the outcome. . . . We're outcome-aware -- but it is about the process that goes [into] it, what happens Monday through Saturday. What happens Monday through Saturday is usually what happens in the game in my experience. That's why you need great week of preparation, but I'm not letting the outcome dictate how I grade the film. I'm grading the film objectively based off of what I see on a weekly basis."

What everyone sees is a team in a tailspin, with the most recent Super Bowl win fading deep into the rear-view mirror. They get their next chance to turn things around on Sunday night, when they face the 1-4-1 Commanders.

Probably Stolen Week 1 walkthrough

The first week is where you begin your trade and set up your shop. First, pick a Specialization from the complete list of options available. Easiest one is Apprentice if you want things to begin smoothly.

Screenshot by Destructoid

Next, pick the starting Perks that you’ll want to shape your save. Similarly, you can pick from between three different difficulty modifier options. The game will give you a quick tutorial of the overlay, and you’re all set then for your first piece of business.

Day 1

There will be five customers to attend, and you can call them using the green button near the recorder. At first, you’ll find your mentor who guides you about the basics. The customers who appear will be randomized, so the ones I encountered will be different from the ones you do.

Ideally, I looked to buy all types of food items since they can be sold for a profit. You’ll also be provided with a list of criminals; if one of them appears, make sure to inform the authorities. This specific encounter is fixed for all players.

Day 2

Screenshot by Destructoid

Again the same pattern follows, and you will have four customers to attend to. Once again, I looked to purchase any food item they had, or discounted stuff they looked to sell for cheap. Do note that not every discounted item will be profitable, but you ultimately go green eventually.

After the second day, you’ll be able to visit the Dumping Ground. Once you exhaust all attempts, pick the most valuable item and return.

Day 3

On the third day, you’ll have six customers to attend. However, one of them will be your mentor, and you’ll learn about how to check discrepancies. You can use your magnifying glass to inspect closely whether the product you’re about to buy has any defects or not.

Day 4

On the fourth day, you’ll come across the labeler. The labeler allows you to fix counterfeit items that raise their values. I also came across one of the six criminals notified on day, and decided to call the authority.

Day 5

Screenshot by Destructoid

On the fifth day, you’ll get to establish your display case. You can keep an assorted list of items in this case. Do note that the types of items will keep will attract different kind of customers to your shop. On my run, a second bounty target appeared, and I once again decided to call the authority on them.

Day 6

The sixth day didn’t have anything worthwhile happening on my run. By now, you’ll find some really valuable items to purchase from few customers. If you have the credits, don’t hesitate to buy.

Day 7

The final day of the first week has the highest number of clients to meet. Moreover, you’ll get to encounter someone named Wild, and you’ll learn about upgrades. Known as Wild’s Favours, you can pick from a wide variety of upgrades.

Your picks will largely depend on how your run is proceeding, and what kind of items you need.

Stay tuned for the walkthrough for the second week and all its days.
